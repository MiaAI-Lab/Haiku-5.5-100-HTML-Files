Title: Casa Sabbia Terrazzo

Description: A warm, editorial product catalogue for an invented terrazzo atelier. Every stone surface is generated live from about a hundred seeded radial-gradient chips. A large hero slab can be "poured" again or switched to a new blend, and chips scatter on in an animated reveal. Below it, a staggered library of nine sample cards in sand, clay, olive, saffron and lagoon tones can be filtered by tone, reshuffled one at a time and added to a sample box. Didot headlines, Avenir body text, polished sheens and springy micro-interactions give it a crafted, premium feel.

Visual techniques:
- Procedural terrazzo from stacked radial-gradient ellipses with hard colour stops and a seeded PRNG
- Two-lobe chips for irregular aggregate silhouettes, plus tiled micro-speck layers
- Progressive per-frame layer reveal for a "pouring" animation
- Hover light sheen via a translating gradient pseudo-element
- Asymmetric border radius, inset rims and warm layered shadows
- Staggered three-column grid offset and filter-driven staggered entrance animations
- Spring-eased buttons, a spinning reshuffle control and a bumping counter badge
- Low-contrast terrazzo backdrop for the whole page
- prefers-reduced-motion support

Interaction model: In the hero, "Pour again" scatters a fresh arrangement of chips onto the big slab and "Change blend" switches to the next of nine aggregate recipes, updating the tag's name and pour number. Tone filter pills show warm, neutral, cool or bold samples, and the visible cards re-enter with a stagger. Hovering a card lifts it, sweeps a sheen across its slab and reveals a ↻ button that reshuffles that sample's chips. "Request sample" toggles the sample into the box and the header counter updates with a springy bump. Every control is a keyboard-focusable button with a visible focus ring.
