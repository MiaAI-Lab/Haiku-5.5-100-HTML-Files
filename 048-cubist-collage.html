Room IV · Atelier Ferrand

Still Life, Reassembled

A guitar, a newspaper and a bottle of ochre light were sitting on a table, and then someone looked at them from five directions at once. Every plane here is a separate glance. Move them and the table remembers it differently.

The fragments overlap with multiply blending, the way translucent paper does when it is pasted onto paper. Where slate meets ochre, a third colour arrives that nobody painted.
