Title: Molten Hours

Description: A warm, hypnotic lava lamp that fills the whole screen with glowing wax. Blobs of amber and flame-colored wax swell, merge, and pull apart as they heat at the base and cool near the top, with hot cream cores where they touch. Heavy outlined display type and a small frosted control panel make it read like a luxury product page for a lamp you can play with.

Visual techniques:
- Metaball field computed per cell into ImageData on a low-resolution offscreen canvas
- Smooth upscaling to a devicePixelRatio-sized canvas for soft, organic edges
- Multi-stop color ramp mapped from field strength, with halo, rim, and core zones
- Additive blurred bloom pass using canvas filter and lighter compositing
- Temperature-driven buoyancy simulation with damping and wall bounces
- Outlined text via -webkit-text-stroke with mix-blend-mode screen
- Frosted glass control panel with backdrop-filter and custom range styling

Interaction model:
Click or tap anywhere on the lamp to pour in a new bead of wax, which grows in at that spot and joins the convection cycle. The Heat slider changes how strongly the base warms the wax, speeding up or calming the rise and fall. "Settle the wax" resets the lamp to a fresh set of blobs, and a toast appears if the lamp is full.
