Title: Halcyon Bento Spotlight

Description: A sleek product-feature page in the style of a modern bento grid, presented for an invented night camera. A single coloured light follows the cursor across the whole grid, tracing the hairline borders of every tile it passes and softly washing their interiors, while each tile carries its own small live visual: rotating star trails, a dial, a breathing histogram, a counting stat and a filling battery meter. The effect feels like moving a torch across a dark instrument panel.

Visual techniques:
- Global pointer tracking that writes per-card CSS custom properties for a cross-card spotlight
- Border-only glow via a padded pseudo-element with mask-composite exclude/xor
- Hue-shifting HSL spotlight driven by cursor x position
- Generated SVG star-trail arcs with slow CSS rotation
- repeating-conic-gradient tick dial masked to a ring
- Gaussian histogram bars animated with scaleY and expo easing
- IntersectionObserver-triggered counter and meter fill, staggered entrance

Interaction model:
Move the cursor (or drag a finger) over the grid to sweep the spotlight across tiles; the hue changes as you move sideways. Hovering the exposure tile turns its dial and steps to a new shutter value. Tabbing through tiles places the light on the focused card. The "Try a stack" button runs a short simulated capture and refreshes the histogram.
