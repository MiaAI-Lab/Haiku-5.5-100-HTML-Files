Title: Rime, A Frost Study

Description: A quiet winter window at night. Fern-like ice dendrites creep in from the frame toward the centre, branching at the six-fold angles real frost favours and glowing faintly against a deep navy sky. The whole pane brightens and dims on a slow breathing rhythm, and a frosted glass plaque with a short italic text sets the mood. You can seed new crystals, or wipe the glass with a finger and watch the frost slowly fill the cleared path again.

Visual techniques:
- Recursive branching growth on an offscreen accumulation canvas with 60-degree side branches
- Two-pass stroke (soft halo plus bright core) for glow without shadowBlur
- destination-out radial gradients for melting and thawing
- Breath cycle driving canvas alpha, a fog gradient and a CSS custom property
- Backdrop-filter glass plaque with staged rise-in animation
- Inset box-shadow window frame and layered radial-gradient sky
- devicePixelRatio sizing with image-preserving resize

Interaction model:
Tap anywhere to plant a six-armed crystal that grows outward. Press and drag to wipe a clear trail through the frost, which new branches can grow back into. The Thaw button melts the pane and restarts from the edges; Freeze edges sends a new wave of crystals inward.
