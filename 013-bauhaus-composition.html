Eleven primary forms on a twelve-column sheet. Every composition is new, snapped to the grid and printed in overlapping primaries. Touch a form to turn it a quarter, or touch the paper to begin again.

The studio's first exercise pairs each primary with the form it seems to want: the triangle that points, the square that sits, the circle that recedes.
