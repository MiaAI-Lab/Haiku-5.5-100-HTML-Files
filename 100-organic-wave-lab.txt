Title: Fluxion OW-7 Wave Lab

Description: A dark, tactile laboratory instrument for watching organic waveforms. A graphite chassis with screws, LEDs and a bezelled phosphor screen hosts layered SVG curves that swell, ripple and breathe like living tissue. Faders on the side panel reshape the signal in real time, a mode switch turns the same maths into tidal fills, fine membrane strands or wobbling cells, and a probe follows the pointer to press on the culture and let it recover. Amber readouts and a running clock make it feel like a working piece of bench equipment.

Visual techniques:
- Per-frame SVG path generation with Catmull-Rom to cubic Bezier smoothing (open and closed)
- Sum-of-sines wave synthesis with harmonic damping and smoothed parameters
- Three render modes: gradient-filled tides, stroked membranes, polar closed blobs
- SVG pattern grid, Gaussian glow filter via feMerge, scanline and glass overlays
- Gaussian-falloff pointer probe that attracts or repels path points
- Custom-styled range inputs with CSS variable fill tracks for WebKit and Firefox
- Eased preset tweening across all controls, segmented control with aria-pressed
- Skeuomorphic chassis: bevel shadows, CSS screws, blinking LEDs

Interaction model: Drag the faders to change amplitude, frequency, viscosity, number of layers and drift speed, and watch the traces flow into their new shapes. Pick Tide, Membrane or Cell to change how the signal is drawn, or press KELP, LUNG or STORM to glide all the controls to a preset. Moving across the screen moves an amber probe that pulls on the waves or dents the cells, and pressing down makes it stronger.
