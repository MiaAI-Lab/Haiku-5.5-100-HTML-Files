Title: Chromatone Bench Unit

Description: A tactile, warm-lit hardware fantasy: a wood-cheeked synth faceplate with chunky silver knobs, a glowing oscilloscope, and colorful patch cords sagging across the bay. The twist is that the instrument's controls tune its own appearance, so dialing TINT, CHROMA, and VALUE repaints the brushed faceplate while GLOW recolors every LED and trace, letting the visitor design their own unit.

Visual techniques:
- Knobs built as inline SVG with gradients, tick rings, knurling, and rotating caps
- Panel color driven entirely by CSS custom properties in hsl()
- Wood grain and brushed metal from layered repeating-linear-gradients
- Screws, jacks, and LEDs from radial gradients and inset shadows
- Animated SVG oscilloscope path with a wavefolder function and drop-shadow glow
- Patch cords as sagging quadratic Bezier paths with shadow, body, and highlight layers
- Automatic text-color contrast switch based on panel lightness

Interaction model:
Drag a knob up or down (or sideways), scroll the wheel over it, or focus it and use the arrow, Home, and End keys; each knob is an accessible slider. Turning the color knobs repaints the faceplate and accent glow live, while DRIFT and FOLD reshape the scope trace and LED tempo. Tap one jack and then another to patch a new cord between them; tapping the same pair again pulls the cord out.
