Title: Spirograph Studio

Description: A tactile, paper-and-ink take on the classic geared drawing toy. A ghosted mechanism of ring, wheel and pen rolls across a circular plate, laying down a hypotrochoid line by line until the rosette closes on itself. Adjusting the teeth or the pen hole retunes the geometry live, and multiple layers in different inks overprint each other with a multiply blend, producing rich, printerly rosettes on warm drafting paper.

Visual techniques:
- Progressive hypotrochoid plotting on a persistent devicePixelRatio-aware canvas
- Separate overlay canvas animating the ring teeth, rolling wheel, spokes, pen arm and pen tip
- Multiply compositing so layered inks darken where they cross
- Closure detection using the greatest common divisor of ring and wheel teeth
- Layer history re-rendered on resize so drawings survive layout changes
- Graph-paper background from crossed linear gradients and a radial-gradient paper plate
- Custom range inputs with ruler-tick tracks and target thumbs, springy hover easing

Interaction model: Drag the ring, wheel and pen-hole sliders to change the curve; the current layer restarts with the new geometry while earlier layers stay on the paper. Pick an ink swatch to change the pen colour, adjust speed, pause or resume, start a new layer on top, let "Surprise me" choose random settings and ink, or clear everything with "Fresh paper". The progress bar and loop counter show how close the rosette is to closing.
