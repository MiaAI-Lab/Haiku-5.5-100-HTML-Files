Title: Gravitas Observatory

Description: An interactive gravity sandbox in which a glowing grid of light behaves like stretched fabric. Two seeded wells give the first frame depth; each click drops a new mass that sinks in with a spring, sends a shock ripple across the lattice and recolours the bent region from cool cyan to hot rose. Over time heavy bodies drift toward one another and merge into deeper wells, while a frosted observatory panel frames the experiment with calm editorial copy and precise monospace readouts.

Visual techniques:
- Full-screen canvas sized to devicePixelRatio with resize handling and a single requestAnimationFrame loop
- Per-node softened inverse-square displacement from multiple masses plus a pointer probe
- Segment colouring by displacement magnitude, batched into six Path2D strokes
- Radial ripple waves modelled as Gaussian-windowed sine displacement
- Spring-damped mass growth, pairwise attraction, damping and momentum-conserving merges
- Event-horizon rendering with radial-gradient glow and rim stroke
- Frosted glass panel with backdrop-filter, gradient range track and glowing thumb

Interaction model: Click or tap anywhere on the canvas to drop a mass of the weight chosen on the slider; moving the pointer gently dents the grid like a fingertip. Toggle Drift to let wells attract and merge or hold them in place, use Clear wells to reset the fabric, and collapse the panel to see more of the lattice on small screens. The panel reports the number of wells and the current peak warp.
