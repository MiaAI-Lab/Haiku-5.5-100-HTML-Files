Title: Starlings over Saltmarsh

Description: A quiet, painterly simulation of a starling murmuration at sunset. Hundreds of small dark birds, each obeying only local rules of alignment, cohesion and separation, fold into rolling sheets across a plum-to-apricot sky above a reed-lined estuary where the low sun is breaking on the water. Moving the pointer through the flock behaves like a hawk diving in: the birds tear open around it and close behind it. The overlay reads like a naturalist's field note, with a large italic title and a small, warm control pill.

Visual techniques:
- Offscreen canvas background with multi-stop sky gradient, radial sun glow, clipped sun disc, water glints and seeded reed silhouettes
- Boids (alignment, cohesion, separation) with a wandering Lissajous attractor
- Uniform spatial hash grid with typed arrays for neighbour search
- Depth layers that vary speed, size and opacity, batched into three fill passes
- Per-bird sine wing flap on chevron silhouettes
- Pointer predator field with quadratic falloff and custom drawn cursor ring
- Expanding ripple shockwaves that push birds outward
- devicePixelRatio-aware canvas with debounced resize and delta-time stepping
- Glass control pill with custom-styled range slider

Interaction model: Move the mouse or a finger across the sky to scare the flock and watch it split and re-form around you. Click or tap to send an expanding ripple through the birds. Drag the Restless slider to make the flock calmer or more frantic, and press Scatter to burst the whole murmuration outward from its centre.
