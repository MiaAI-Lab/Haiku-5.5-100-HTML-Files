Title: Halcyon Generative EQ

Description: A sleek dark equalizer that listens to music that does not exist. A procedural groove with kick, snare, hats and a drifting melody drives a segmented LED spectrum with falling peak caps and a soft reflection. Ten tactile faders, presets and a tempo control reshape that spectrum live, and a glowing response curve drawn over the bars shows the EQ shape you have set.

Visual techniques:
- Canvas spectrum with rounded LED segments coloured from a precomputed multi-stop gradient
- Procedural rhythm energy model (kick, snare, hat and melody envelopes plus value noise)
- Catmull-Rom interpolation of ten band gains into a smooth response curve
- Attack and release smoothing and gravity-driven peak caps
- Mirrored low-alpha reflection with a gradient floor fade
- Glowing curve rendered with canvas shadowBlur and a dashed reference line
- Custom accessible vertical sliders with pointer capture and keyboard control
- Eased preset tweening and gradient-clipped title text
- CSS LED indicators whose blink rate is synced to tempo through a custom property

Interaction model: Drag any of the ten faders up or down, or focus one and use the arrow keys, to boost or cut that band. The bars and response curve react instantly. Preset chips glide the whole fader bank into a new shape, Surprise invents a random curve, double-clicking a fader resets it to 0 dB, and the Tempo slider changes the groove's speed, the bar counter and the beat LED.
