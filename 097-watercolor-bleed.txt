Title: Wet on Wet

Description: A quiet, paper-toned page about watercolour where the colour itself is made from SVG filters rather than images. Translucent washes of blue, rose, yellow and green overlap behind an oversized italic title, their edges torn and granulated by turbulence noise and rimmed with darker pigment the way a drying wash pools at its border. Four swatch cards present a spring palette; each one blooms outward and reveals a note on the pigment's character when the viewer hovers, so the page appears to rewet under the pointer and dry again when left alone.

Visual techniques:
- SVG feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap chains for ragged, organic wash edges
- Arithmetic feComposite and a noise-derived alpha mask for granulation
- feMorphology erode with composite "out" to fake the dark drying rim
- mix-blend-mode multiply for glaze-like overlaps and type over paint
- Full-page fractal-noise paper grain overlay
- JavaScript-eased filter attributes (displacement scale, base frequency) animated per frame
- Generated per-swatch filters and radial gradients with unique ids
- Drop cap and multi-column essay layout

Interaction model: Moving the pointer across the hero makes the wash bleed further and shifts its texture with the cursor, then it slowly settles when the pointer leaves. Hovering, focusing or tapping a swatch rewets it: the edge spreads, the blot swells and a short pigment note appears. A click keeps a swatch wet until clicked again. A status label in the legend reports whether any paint on the page is still moving.
