Title: Kinetica Week

Description: A loud, poster-like festival page built from stacked, full-bleed bands of giant condensed type that run in alternating directions. The bands idle gently, then surge, lean and reverse as the visitor scrolls, so the act of reading the page becomes the throttle. Bold flat colour bands, an outlined-type row and an italic serif row keep the rhythm varied, and short editorial copy about the invented festival gives the motion a reason to exist.

Visual techniques:
- JavaScript marquee tracks with cloned units, measured widths and seamless modulo wrapping
- Scroll velocity sampling per animation frame with eased smoothing driving speed, direction and skew
- -webkit-text-stroke outlined display type alongside filled and italic serif rows
- Spinning inline SVG star separators
- Conic-gradient velocity dial and live multiplier readout
- mix-blend-mode difference fixed header readable over every band
- Springy cubic-bezier CTA hover with rotation
- Resize-aware re-layout of marquee clones

Interaction model: Scroll the page: the faster you scroll, the faster every band runs and the harder the type leans, and scrolling up reverses all directions. Hover or keyboard-focus a band to slow it nearly to a halt and highlight it. The Freeze button in the velocity gauge stops all bands until released, and the closing CTA confirms a reserved pass when pressed.
