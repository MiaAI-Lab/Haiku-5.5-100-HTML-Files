Title: Hearth Soft Thermostat

Description: A tactile, calm thermostat rendered entirely in soft extruded and recessed surfaces. The ring glows from cool blue to ember orange as you set the warmth, the needle springs into place with a satisfying wobble, and the numerals roll alongside it. Small details such as the lit tick marks, glowing status dot and pressed-in buttons make the object feel physical and premium.

Visual techniques:
- Neumorphism with paired light and dark outer shadows and inset shadows for wells, pills and pressed states
- Inline SVG ticks, arcs and knob generated in JavaScript with polar-to-cartesian math
- Blurred duplicate arc as a soft color glow, SVG drop-shadow filters on the knob
- Live accent color interpolation shared through a CSS custom property
- Semi-implicit spring integration with substeps for a stable, bouncy needle
- Rolling numeric readout derived from the needle position

Interaction model: Drag around the dial to set a temperature, or use the plus and minus buttons, or focus the dial and use the arrow, Page and Home/End keys. Choosing Heat, Eco or Cool applies a preset and updates the status. The needle springs toward each new setting, the arc and ticks recolor, and the footer updates the estimated time to reach the target while the room reading slowly drifts toward it.
