Title: The Meridian Walk

Description: A premium, tactile scroll piece in which a hand-folded pocket guide hangs from its navy cover over a dark desk and opens panel by panel into a true 3D accordion as the reader scrolls. - Relative rotateX per fold computed from alternating absolute tilts (absolute minus parent)
- Staggered cubic in-out easing mapped from scroll progress through a sticky stage
- Normal-based lighting using CSS custom properties for shade and highlight overlays
- Paper fibre texture from layered repeating-linear-gradients
- A continuous SVG dotted route split across panels with matching edge coordinates
- Blurred contact shadow that scales with the unfold progress
- Hover underline draw-in, rotating seal and an animated scroll hint hairline

Interaction model: Scrolling is the main control. Moving down the page unfolds the guide one crease at a time, and scrolling back up refolds it. Each panel is a focusable button: clicking it or pressing Enter smooth-scrolls to the moment that panel lies flat, and the cover folds the guide back up. Two pill buttons jump to fully unfolded or fully folded, while a segmented meter and a monospace readout report how many panels are open and how far along the walk the reader is.
