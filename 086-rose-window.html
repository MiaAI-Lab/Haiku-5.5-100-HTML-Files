The Rose of the Hours

Glazed c. 1241 · thirty-seven lights

†

A wheel of light

Read the window as the monks did

At the heart sits the oculus, the eye of morning. Around it turn the twelve signs of heaven; beyond them, in tall lancets, the twelve labours of the year; between the lancets, the virtues that hold the wheel together. Select any light to hear what it was made to say.

Move across the glass to carry the sun. Tab and Enter work too.

Lead, iron, pot-metal glass · restored in the year of the long frost
