Title: Selenographic Almanac

Description: A printed-almanac style lunar calendar in pale parchment and indigo ink. Every night of the month is drawn as an accurate miniature SVG phase computed from the synodic cycle, while a large, finely textured moon on the left shows the selected night, its terminator sweeping smoothly to the new phase. Didone headlines, invented moon names, small gold Full and New tags, and a short verse for each phase give it the feel of a collectible reference page.

Visual techniques:
- Single-path SVG phase rendering from an outer arc plus an elliptical terminator arc
- clipPath that confines maria and crater details to the sunlit part of the large moon
- Radial gradients for the lit limb and the shadowed disc, illumination-scaled drop-shadow glow
- requestAnimationFrame tween of the phase fraction along the shortest path with quartic easing
- Hairline almanac grid, Didone type, rotating dashed orbit ring, fade-in month transitions
- Springy hover scale and tilt on the small moons

Interaction model: Readers click any night, or use the arrow keys, to see that night's moon rendered large with its phase name, illumination, age, and countdown to the next full or new moon. The arrow buttons page through months and Today snaps back to the present. Keyboard focus is clearly marked, and reduced-motion users get instant, still updates.
