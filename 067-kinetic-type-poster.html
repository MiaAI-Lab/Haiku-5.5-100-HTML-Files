Hall 9, Old Boiler Works14 — 16 March
Tracking ±0Hover a line to let it breathe

Manifesto

Space is a material. A word set tight shouts; the same word opened up begins to whisper. For three days we pull letters apart and push them together until the gaps start talking.

Programme

  1. Counterspace, a lecture in negative shapes
  2. The Kerning Table, open workshop
  3. Wood type night print, 22:00 until dawn
  4. Last word: a poster wall for everyone

Admission

Free entry, all weights welcome. Bring a word you want to hear louder. Doors open at ten, the presses stop when the ink runs out.
