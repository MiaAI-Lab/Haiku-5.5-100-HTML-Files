2. LEAN: "Letters tilt toward a wind they cannot see."
3. HEAVY: "Meaning gathers weight the way rain gathers on roofs."
4. THIN: "Then it thins, a hairline of ink across the dusk."
5. RUSH: "Faster now; the sentence outruns its speaker."
6. REST: "And settles, upright, into the quiet it came from."

The displayed word swaps at the midpoint, letters dropping in with a staggered blur-and-rise keyframe (55ms per letter). Scroll velocity adds extra skew that grows letter by letter, so fast scrolling makes the word lean like it is being dragged. In RUSH the whole stage floods vermilion (#ff4a1c) with ink-black type, and a sine wave ripples weight and baseline across the letters.

Palette: ink #0f0e0c, bone #efe8da, vermilion accent #ff4a1c, with slight warm and cool shifts per stanza. A faint dotted grain overlay (two radial-gradient dot grids in overlay blend) and a soft vignette sit on the stage.

Title: Tidewords, Six Weights

Description: A kinetic typographic poem in which a single giant word occupies a sticky stage and is physically reshaped by scrolling. As the reader descends, the word moves from a hushed hairline STILL to a leaning, italicised LEAN, a swollen black HEAVY, a hollow outlined THIN, a skewed, rippling RUSH on a flood of vermilion, and finally an upright, settled REST. Each word is accompanied by a line of serif italic verse, and the whole stage shifts its background and ink colour between stanzas so the poem reads as a sequence of moods rather than a list of words.

Visual techniques:
- Sticky full-viewport stage inside a tall scroll track
- Per-letter spans with JavaScript-driven font-weight, skewX, translateY and colour
- Smoothstep-plateau interpolation between stanza parameter sets, plus per-frame easing
- Scroll velocity mapped to cumulative per-letter skew
- Hollow outline type with -webkit-text-stroke and transparent fill for THIN
- Background and foreground RGB interpolation via CSS custom properties
- Staggered blur-and-rise keyframe for each word swap
- Dotted grain overlay and radial vignette built from gradients
- Progress rule scaled with transform, dot rail navigation with slide-in labels
- prefers-reduced-motion handling that removes lag, wave and velocity skew

Interaction model: The reader simply scrolls; scroll position chooses the stanza and blends typography between states, and scroll speed bends the letters. The dot rail (right edge on desktop, bottom row on phones) jumps smoothly to any stanza by click or keyboard, showing the stanza word on hover and focus. At the end, a colophon offers a "Read it again" button that scrolls back to the opening stanza.
