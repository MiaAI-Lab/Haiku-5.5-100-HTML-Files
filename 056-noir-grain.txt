Title: The Last Tram to Halvard

Description: A cinematic title sequence rendered entirely in silver and black. Letterbox bars part to reveal a rain-soaked street at midnight, a lone silhouette under a lamppost and slatted window light falling across the scene. Animated film grain, gate flicker and stray scratches give the illusion of projected nitrate stock, while a framed intertitle card carries a short five-card noir story with invented cast and crew credits. The key light drifts after the pointer like a spotlight operator following the viewer.

Visual techniques:
- Low-resolution canvas noise regenerated at film frame rate, upscaled with pixelated rendering and overlay blending for grain
- Grayscale + contrast filter on the whole stage for a unified silver-gelatin look
- Venetian-blind light from a skewed repeating-linear-gradient with an elliptical CSS mask driven by custom properties
- CSS-only street scene: gradients, pseudo-element lamp halo, inline SVG silhouette
- Letterbox bars as fixed pseudo-elements animating from closed to 12vh
- Stepped keyframe exposure flicker and random vertical film scratches
- Animated diagonal rain via moving repeating gradients
- Card transitions with opacity, scale and blur cross-fade, live SMPTE timecode

Interaction model: Move the pointer to steer the key light and blind shadows across the scene. Click anywhere, press Space or the Right Arrow, or use the "Next card" button to advance to the next intertitle; Left Arrow goes back. Reel dots in the bottom letterbox show progress through the five cards, and the final card loops back to the title.
