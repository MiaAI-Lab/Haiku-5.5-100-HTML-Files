Title: Aurora Glass

Description: A calm, premium night-sky landing page for an imaginary Arctic observatory. Slow aurora ribbons drift behind a starfield and a silhouetted ridge, while frosted glass cards float in front, catching the light. The serif headline with its gradient italic word sets an editorial, luxurious tone, and the cards present the night's forecast with large numerals and soft gradient meters.

Visual techniques:
- Blurred linear-gradient aurora ribbons with mix-blend-mode screen and long keyframe drift
- Starfield built purely from stacked radial gradients with a twinkle animation
- Glassmorphism cards using backdrop-filter blur and saturate, translucent borders and inset highlights
- CSS custom properties driven by JavaScript for 3D tilt and pointer-tracked radial glare
- preserve-3d with translateZ content for parallax depth inside each card
- Gradient-clipped italic serif text and scaleX meter fill animation
- Inline SVG mountain ridge silhouette

Interaction model: Moving a mouse or pen across a card tilts it toward the pointer in 3D and slides a soft glare highlight to match, throttled through requestAnimationFrame. Leaving the card eases it back to flat. Cards can be reached with the keyboard and show a focus ring. Touch users get the static layout without tilt, and reduced-motion users get a still sky.
