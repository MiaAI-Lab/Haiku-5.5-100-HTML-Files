Three buildings that refuse to apologise. Board-marked, raw and heavy with weather, they still carry the grain of the timber that held them while they set. Light is the only thing that changes them.

I

The Stepped Hall

Civic Archive, Haldane Quay

Each floor reaches a little further than the one beneath it, so the building shades its own doorway. In August the lower terrace is the coolest room in the city, and the archivists eat lunch there in silence.

II

The Coffered Wall

Weller House, residential block

Forty-eight deep window boxes, each one a small room of shadow. Residents say they can tell the hour by how far the dark has crept across the sills. Select a window to switch its lamp on.

III

The Tower and Its Shadow

Signal Tower, Ostmark Ridge

Fourteen storeys of vertical fins with nothing at the top but a water tank and a view. At either end of the day its shadow is the longest thing in the valley, reaching the river by dusk.
