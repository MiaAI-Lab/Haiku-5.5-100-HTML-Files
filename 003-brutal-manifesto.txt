Title: BLUNT Manifesto

Description: A loud, confident neo-brutalist manifesto page that celebrates exposed structure. Thick borders, a single hard shadow direction, oversized black type and a four-color palette create a poster-like grid, while a constant headline ticker gives it the energy of a protest banner. Interactions are tactile and stepped, so every press feels like a stamp landing on paper.

Visual techniques:
- Thick 4px borders and unblurred offset box-shadows as the core visual vocabulary
- CSS grid with a 12-column layout collapsing to 6 and 1 columns
- Infinite marquee ticker using duplicated content and a translateX(-50%) loop
- Outlined numerals using -webkit-text-stroke with transparent fill
- Stepped transitions (steps()) and overshoot cubic-bezier for the stamp pop
- Progress bars using scaleX transforms with mix-blend-mode difference labels
- Faint grid paper background from layered linear gradients

Interaction model: Click or press Enter or Space on any rule card to agree with it; the card flips to blue, stamps "AGREED", and the agreement bar steps forward until all six are accepted. The "Sign it" button presses physically into the page, rolls the signature counter up by one, advances the goal bar and locks into a signed state. Hovering the ticker pauses it, and every interactive element has a dashed focus outline.
