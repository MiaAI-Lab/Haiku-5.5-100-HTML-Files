Title: Squishworks Clay Studio

Description: A tactile, pastel claymorphism showcase where six soft 3D blobs sit on a warm putty surface like freshly kneaded modelling clay. Layered inner and outer shadows sell the volume, organic border-radius values give each blob its own lumpy silhouette, and a hand-rolled spring simulation makes every hover and tap feel physical. A clay tray at the bottom tallies squishes and reports the clay's mood, turning a simple shading study into a small, joyful toy.

Visual techniques:
- Claymorphism via stacked inset box-shadows (tinted shade plus white highlight) and soft drop shadows
- Organic eight-value border-radius shapes, randomized in JS and eased with CSS transitions
- Blurred, rotated pseudo-element specular highlights
- Extruded headline using stacked text-shadows
- Custom JS spring physics on scaleX, scaleY and rotation with bottom transform origin
- Inset groove progress meter with overshoot cubic-bezier fill
- Blinking eyes via scaleY on squish
- Responsive grid that collapses from three to two columns

Interaction model: Hovering or focusing a blob makes it squash and settle with a springy wobble; clicking or pressing Enter squishes it flat, blinks its eyes, and may give it a new shape as it bounces back. Each squish increments the counter, fills the meter, and updates the mood text. The "Reshape the clay" button remolds all six blobs in a staggered cascade. Idle blobs occasionally jiggle on their own unless reduced motion is requested.
