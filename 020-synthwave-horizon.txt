Title: Nightdrive, Radio Halcyon

Description: A retro-futurist synthwave postcard you can drive. A slatted gradient sun sinks behind neon wireframe mountains while a magenta grid races toward the viewer along an endless perspective floor, all held together by a glowing cyan horizon line with a scanning highlight. A chrome-lettered NIGHTDRIVE title with a neon script "forever" floats above the sun, and a monospace HUD reads out the cruising speed. Steering banks the whole horizon, boosting turns the stars into hyperspace streaks, and three "cassette" buttons swap the entire scene between Sunset, Midnight and Acid colourways.

Visual techniques:
- CSS 3D perspective floor with rotateX plane and JavaScript-animated background-position grid
- mask-image gradient slats on a gradient sun, with a slow CSS set-and-rise keyframe
- Inline SVG wireframe mountains with non-scaling neon strokes and drop-shadow glow
- Glowing horizon line with box-shadow bloom and a scanning pseudo-element highlight
- Chrome gradient text via background-clip: text with a horizon band, plus neon script text-shadow
- Canvas starfield sized to devicePixelRatio that turns into radial streaks at speed
- Theme swapping through CSS custom properties and a data-theme attribute, using color-mix for glows
- Parallax and camera bank driven by an eased steering value
- Scanline overlay and stepped channel-change flash

Interaction model: Move the pointer left and right (or press the arrow keys) to steer: the horizon banks, the grid slides and the mountains and sun shift in parallax. Hold the mouse button, a finger, or the Space bar to boost, which speeds up the grid, raises the speed readout and stretches the stars into streaks; let go to coast back to cruising speed. Click Sunset, Midnight or Acid to switch the colour cassette.
