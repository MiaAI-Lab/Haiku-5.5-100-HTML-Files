Title: Evening Bellflower Herbarium

Description: A sepia museum herbarium sheet for a fictional dusk-flowering bellflower. A hand-drawn inline SVG plant draws itself root to bloom on a foxed paper sheet, with gummed tape, a typed accession label and an ink stamp. Red numbered pins mark ten features, and each opens a collector's field note in a card beside the plate, so the page feels like reading a 19th-century specimen with a curator next to you.

Visual techniques:
- Inline SVG botanical illustration with pattern-fill hatching for engraved shading
- stroke-dasharray / stroke-dashoffset line drawing on load
- Staggered opacity keyframes for leaves, flowers and buds
- Layered radial and linear gradients for paper fibers, foxing and vignette
- Script-generated SVG pins with dashed leader lines and a pulsing halo keyframe
- Springy CSS transition on the SVG circle radius
- Cross-fading annotation card with an aria-live region
- Rotated monospace specimen label and a circular stamp
- prefers-reduced-motion fallback that shows the finished drawing statically

Interaction model: The viewer hovers, taps or tabs through ten numbered markers on the plant, or uses the matching numbered index buttons. The active marker turns solid red, its leader line appears and a halo pulses, while the field-notes card cross-fades to that feature's name and the collector's observation. Everything works by keyboard (Enter or Space on a focused pin) and by touch.
