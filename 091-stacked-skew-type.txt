Title: Momentum Stack

Description: A letterpress-flavored typographic broadside in which eight rows of massive grotesque words hang on springs. Scroll velocity, not scroll position, shears the stack: rows lean in alternating directions in proportion to how hard the page is flung, stretch slightly taller under strain, and settle with a damped wobble once motion stops. Newsprint, black ink, and a single vermilion accent keep it austere, while outlined words, italic margin notes, and a live velocity meter add editorial detail.

Visual techniques:
- Scroll-velocity sampling in requestAnimationFrame fed into a damped spring simulation
- CSS custom properties (--skew, --stretch, per-word --k) driving skewX and scaleY transforms
- Outlined display type via -webkit-text-stroke alongside solid and accent fills
- Ink-flood hover using a scaleY pseudo-element with a custom cubic-bezier
- Fixed repeating-linear-gradient paper texture with multiply blending
- Multi-column serif essay with a colored drop cap
- Animated "drip" scroll hint and live velocity meter

Interaction model: The reader scrolls; the faster they scroll, the more the stacked words shear and stretch, and when they stop the type wobbles back upright. Hovering or tabbing to a row inverts it to paper-on-ink and reveals a marginal note. The "Give it a shove" button kicks the spring so the stack sways without scrolling. With reduced motion enabled, the type stays upright.
