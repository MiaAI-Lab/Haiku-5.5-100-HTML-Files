Note colors: sunflower #ffe46b, mint #b4efc6, rose #ffb6c9, sky #a8dbff and tangerine #ffc580, each with a white sheen gradient, a darker bottom edge and an asymmetric bottom-right radius that suggests a lifted, curling corner. Interaction: pointer-drag any note. On pickup it rises to the top, scales up 6% and gets a deep lifted shadow. Reduced motion removes tilt, scaling and spring transitions.

Title: Studio Wren Launch Wall

Description: A tactile corkboard in a wooden frame, where pastel sticky notes held by tape or pushpins carry a small studio's real to-dos in a handwritten hand. Picking a note up lifts it off the cork with a deep shadow and a tilt that follows your hand, and letting go springs it back into place. A frosted toolbar adds new notes in five paper colors, a tidy button snaps everything into a grid, and a little bin swallows discarded notes with a crumple animation.

Visual techniques:
- Layered radial-gradient speckle cork texture with an SVG feTurbulence grain overlay
- Wooden frame built from stacked inset box-shadows
- Paper notes with sheen gradients, an asymmetric corner radius for curl, and clip-path torn tape
- Radial-gradient glossy pushpins
- Transform-based positioning with spring cubic-bezier transitions and velocity-driven tilt
- Crumple-into-bin keyframes driven by a CSS custom property target
- Backdrop-filter frosted toolbar, toast notifications
- Fractional coordinates for resize-safe layout, with localStorage persistence

Interaction model: Drag notes anywhere on the board; they tilt as you move them and settle when dropped. Drop a note on the bin to throw it away. Pick a color and press "+ Note" to add one, then type straight into it; double-click, the pencil button or Enter edits an existing note. "Tidy" lines everything up. With the keyboard, Tab moves between notes, arrow keys nudge them, and Delete removes one.
