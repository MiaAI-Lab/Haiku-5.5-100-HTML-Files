Title: Orbital Ring Clock

Description: A strict, airy Swiss poster that is also a working clock. Three concentric rings fill clockwise for hours, minutes and seconds, a single red point orbits the outer ring once a minute, and a monumental Helvetica readout, a world-zone list and a legend row sit on a rigorous 12-column grid. Selecting a ring brings its value into the centre of the dial, and choosing a city re-times the whole composition.

Visual techniques:
- Inline SVG rings with pathLength=100 and stroke-dasharray progress arcs
- Script-generated tick marks and numerals placed with trigonometry
- Millisecond-smooth orbiting marker dots and a dasharray trail segment
- Intl.DateTimeFormat formatToParts for offline multi-timezone time
- 12-column CSS grid layout with heavy and hairline rules in Swiss style
- Eased stroke-width focus states and an animated red underline on list items
- Tabular numerals and tight negative tracking for display figures
- Reduced-motion mode that steps once per second instead of animating per frame

Interaction model: Hover a ring to read its value in the hub, or click or tab to a ring to keep it selected. Pick a city from the numbered list to show that zone's time on the dial, the digital readout, the date and the legend. Toggle between 24-hour and 12-hour display. Everything works by mouse, touch and keyboard.
