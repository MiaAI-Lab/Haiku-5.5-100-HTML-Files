Title: The Marlow Evening Courier

Description: A lovingly typeset front page of a fictional seaside broadsheet, printed on warm newsprint and laid on a dark desk. A blackletter masthead crowns justified multi-column copy about a sea that has become unusually polite, complete with a drop cap, an engraved lighthouse illustration, side briefs about a stubborn clock tower and queueing gulls, and a weather box. Swapping editions re-presses the headline and stamps it in red, while a wire ticker crawls along the bottom of the screen.

Visual techniques:
- CSS multi-column layout with column rules, justified and hyphenated text, and a first-letter drop cap
- Layered radial and repeating-linear gradients for newsprint texture and a fold line
- Inline SVG engraving built from hatched strokes and flat ink shapes
- Keyframed press-in animation with overshoot and a rotated stamp slam
- Background-size underline draw on hover for brief headlines
- Seamless infinite marquee with duplicated content, paused on hover or focus
- Responsive grid that collapses columns at narrow widths
- Reduced-motion handling for ticker speed and press effects

Interaction model: Readers pick Morning, Late City or Extra in the dateline to swap the lead story, which re-prints with a press and stamp effect. Sidebar briefs are buttons that expand or collapse with a click, Enter or Space. Hovering or focusing the wire ticker pauses it so a headline can be read.
