SUMI

a study in the weight of ink

Ensō no. 1 · tap to paint again

i

First Light

Grind the stick slowly. The stone remembers every morning you hurried, and the ink comes out thin with impatience.

ii

The Long Noon

A single stroke is not drawn; it is let go. The wrist decides, the paper agrees, and the hand learns to stay out of the way.

iii

Dusk Wash

Water carries the black into grey, and grey into fog. Whatever you leave unpainted becomes the mountain you were trying to describe.

iv

Night Ink

Rest the brush bristles-down, never upright. By morning it will hold the shape of what you meant tonight.

Kōyō Paper Works · hand-laid kozo, 42 g/m² · one colour, one breath
