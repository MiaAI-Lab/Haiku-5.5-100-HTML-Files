Halcyon EQ-10

A ten-band graphic equalizer listening to a song that does not exist. The groove is generated on the fly; your faders shape what it looks like.

SignalBeat
BAR 001 · 1GEN · 48 kHz · NO AUDIO

Drag a fader, or focus one and use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps, 0 to reset). The thin line on the screen is the response curve you have drawn; the bars are the generated groove passed through it.
