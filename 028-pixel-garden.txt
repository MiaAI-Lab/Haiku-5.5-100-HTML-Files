Title: Plotlet Pixel Garden

Description: A cozy 8-bit pocket garden rendered at true low resolution and scaled up crisply. A full day passes in about a minute: the dithered sky moves from indigo through coral dawn to bright noon and violet dusk, the sun and moon trade places, and the whole scene dims to moonlight. Visitors sow four kinds of flowers that grow in visible stages, respond to the light by opening and closing, and add small details like sun-tracking sunflowers, glowing bluebells and night fireflies, all framed by chunky pixel UI.

Visual techniques:
- Per-pixel ImageData rendering on a 96 by 64 buffer, integer-scaled with image smoothing disabled
- Keyframed sky color interpolation with banding and 2 by 2 Bayer ordered dithering
- Global light-level shading with a blue night tint
- Sun and moon on opposite arcs, with blended halo and crescent cut-out
- Procedural sine hills and noise-table soil texture
- Staged procedural plant sprites with species-specific behaviors
- Seed fall and dust particles, wandering blinking fireflies
- Pixel-art UI borders built from hard box-shadows and stepped text-shadows

Interaction model: Click or tap anywhere over the garden to drop a seed of the selected kind, which falls to the soil and starts growing; hovering highlights where it will land. Seed packet buttons pick the species, the time button cycles day speed between 1x, 3x and 8x, and "Till soil" clears the plot back to the empty state. The HUD updates the clock, phase and bloom count live. Keyboard users can focus the canvas and press Enter or Space to plant at a random spot.
