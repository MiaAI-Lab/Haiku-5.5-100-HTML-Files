Title: Kaisekitei Dry Garden

Description: A full-screen raked sand garden framed in dark wood. Fine, grainy sand is combed into straight parallel lines that swirl into concentric rings around five mossy stones. Dragging the pointer pulls a multi-tine rake through the sand, carving embossed grooves that follow the path smoothly, and tapping a stone resettles the sand around it in rings. Small italic phrases surface and fade as the viewer works, and a few red petals drift across the bed, making the page feel calm, tactile and unhurried.

Visual techniques:
- Offscreen canvas as a persistent drawing surface composited each frame
- Procedural grain tile used through createPattern for both base fill and smoothing
- Embossed grooves from paired offset strokes (shadow and highlight)
- Normal-vector tine offsets with smoothed normals for continuous multi-line raking
- Pre-rendered stone sprites with irregular quadratic-curve outlines, radial shading, shadowBlur and moss speckles
- Elliptical concentric ring generation and expanding feedback ripple
- Frosted backdrop-filter toolbar and vertical writing-mode title
- devicePixelRatio scaling, debounced resize that preserves the drawing, dirty-flag rendering under reduced motion

Interaction model: Drag anywhere on the sand to rake it; the number of tines is chosen from the toolbar. Tapping a stone rakes fresh rings around it with a soft ripple. Smooth wipes the bed to plain sand for free drawing, and Re-rake restores the classic lines and rings. Short contemplative messages appear after longer strokes and actions.
