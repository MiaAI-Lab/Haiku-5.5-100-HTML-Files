Title: Platonic Catalogue

Description: A dark, gallery-plate presentation of the five Platonic solids. A single wireframe solid tumbles slowly in the centre of a black stage, its edges drawn in warm paper white with depth shading and its vertices picked out in vermilion. Editorial italic serif names, a monospace data plate, an outlined Roman numeral and a quiet five-column index give it the feel of a printed museum catalogue brought to life. Each click collapses the current solid to a point and blooms the next one out of it.

Visual techniques:
- Canvas 2D perspective projection of 3D vertices with manual rotation matrices
- Procedurally generated Platonic solid geometry (golden-ratio coordinates) with automatic edge detection
- Depth-based alpha and line-width shading for edges and vertices
- Eased cubic collapse-and-bloom morph with twist between shapes
- Outlined display numerals with -webkit-text-stroke
- devicePixelRatio-aware canvas with resize handling and requestAnimationFrame loop
- Hairline grid footer index with aria-pressed active state

Interaction model: The solid rotates on its own. Drag on the stage to spin it with momentum that relaxes back to the idle drift; a click or tap without dragging transmutes it into the next solid. The footer buttons jump straight to any of the five solids, and with the canvas focused, Enter, Space and the arrow keys cycle through them. Hovering slows the rotation so the structure can be studied.
