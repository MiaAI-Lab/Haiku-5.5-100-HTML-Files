Title: The Lamplighter's Daughter

Description: A dark theatre programme seen through a moving spotlight. Red velvet curtains with a gold-fringed valance part to reveal an elegant Didone-set programme: title, cast list with dotted leaders, a three-act scene guide and a note from the director. Everything outside a warm circular beam stays in near darkness, so the reader literally holds the lamp, echoing the story of a lamplighter on the last night of gas light. A house-lights button brings the whole page up for comfortable reading.

Visual techniques:
- Fixed radial-gradient darkness overlay driven by CSS custom properties updated in a rAF loop
- Screen-blended warm light cone under the darkness
- Eased pointer following, press-to-widen, subtle flicker and Lissajous idle drift
- Velvet curtains and scalloped valance from layered repeating and radial gradients with gold fringes
- Theatrical cubic-bezier curtain opening on load
- Dotted leader lines via flex ordering and a pseudo-element
- Perspective-rotated floorboard strip and gold hairline ornament
- House-lights toggle with aria-pressed and focus-following spotlight

Interaction model: After the curtains open, the spotlight follows the pointer or finger with a slight lag and widens while pressed. Tabbing through the page moves the beam to the focused element. If the visitor stops moving, the light drifts slowly around the stage on its own. The house-lights button fades the darkness away for full reading and can dim it again.
