The guide is seven nested .fold elements. Each fold is panel-height (clamp(66px, 10.6vh, 112px)), has transform-origin at its top edge, uses transform-style: preserve-3d, and holds the next fold absolutely positioned at top:100%. Panel 0 is the navy cover (title, "3.8 miles · seven stops · est. 1931", a round "K&F" seal that rotates on hover). Panels i to vi are the stops: The Ropewalk 0.0 mi, Salt Steps 0.6, Chandler's Row 1.3, The Tide Clock 2.0, Lantern Quay 2.9, Home by the Ferry 3.8. Each has a short evocative line. Motion: JavaScript reads scroll progress p (0 to 1) through the track and sets a --r rotateX custom property on each fold. Each panel i has an absolute tilt that alternates in sign, (+/-)80deg * (1 - s_i), where s_i is a cubic in-out eased, staggered unfold value (start offset 0.62 per panel). Because the folds are nested, the relative rotation written to each one is its absolute tilt minus its parent's. This gives a true zig-zag accordion that opens top-down. Lighting comes from the face normal: panels tilted away get a darker multiply overlay (--shade), panels tilted toward the viewer get a cream highlight (--lite). A soft blurred contact shadow under the guide grows in scaleY with progress. Updates are throttled with requestAnimationFrame on scroll.

Details: the panel heading gets a vermilion underline that draws in on hover or focus, and the route dot turns vermilion. Buttons invert to cream on hover and press to scale .96. The progress meter segments fill per panel, and the readout updates the miles walked. A colophon after the track: a vermilion gradient rule, the italic line "Fold it back along the creases it already knows.", and a printer's note.

Interaction: scroll to unfold. Click a panel (or press Enter or Space on it) to smooth-scroll to the point where that panel has just opened; clicking the cover folds everything. "Unfold all" and "Fold up" scroll to the end and the start of the track.

Reduced motion: the track collapses to auto height, the stage is no longer sticky, the guide renders fully unfolded, smooth scrolling and the hint animation are off, and transitions are near zero.
