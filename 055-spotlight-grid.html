In the darkroom you work by a red lamp that the paper cannot see. Everything waits in the tray: a face, a harbour, the corner of a room you will not stand in again.

Rock the bath slowly. First the shadows arrive, then the edges of a window, then the light itself, which was there the whole time and only needed patience. Rinse. Hang it on the line. Let the silver settle.
