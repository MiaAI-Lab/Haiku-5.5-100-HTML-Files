Title: A Year of Night

Description:
A refined, dark analytics piece that turns a fictional observatory's logbook into a glowing contribution-style calendar. Squares shade from cool indigo to ember and gold, so the year's clear spells and meteor showers burn visibly out of the grid. A sliding pill switches between three invented datasets, the grid re-inks in a rolling wave, and every square carries a small human note from the ledger.

Visual techniques:
- CSS grid calendar that transposes from week-columns to week-rows at phone width
- Seeded PRNG data generation with seasonal curves and Gaussian meteor-shower peaks
- Six-step indigo-to-gold sequential colour scale via CSS custom properties
- Staggered transition-delay wave reveal and recolour with spring easing
- Sliding gradient pill tab indicator measured from button offsets
- Ease-out count-up numbers and an animated monthly bar sparkline
- Glass tooltip with viewport clamping, backdrop blur, and month-isolation dimming
- Roving keyboard focus across the grid with arrow, Home, and End keys

Interaction model:
Hover or tap any square to see that night's date, value, and ledger note. Hover a month label to dim everything except that month. Click or arrow between the tabs to switch metrics; the grid recolours and the stats count to new values. Keyboard users can tab into the grid and move day by day with the arrow keys, with the tooltip following focus.
