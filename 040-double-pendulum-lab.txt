Title: Chaos Bench Pendulum

Description: A precise, instrument-grade simulation of a double pendulum set on a dark engineering grid. A lime bob whips through looping arcs, leaving a luminous trail that brightens toward cyan when it moves fast, while a side rail of hairline sliders lets the visitor change arm lengths, mass ratio, gravity, and trail length. A twin pendulum and a live phase portrait make the butterfly effect visible: two nearly identical starts that soon trace completely different paths.

Visual techniques:
- RK4 numerical integration of the double-pendulum equations of motion with substeps
- devicePixelRatio-aware canvas with resize handling and a single requestAnimationFrame loop
- Fading, velocity-colored trail built from per-segment alpha, width, and HSL hue
- Glowing bobs via canvas shadowBlur on a grid background made from CSS gradients
- Secondary phase-space canvas that accumulates and slowly fades plotted points
- Custom range inputs with CSS-variable progress fills and rotated diamond thumbs
- Live numeric readouts for time, angles, and conserved energy

Interaction model:
Drag either bob on the stage to pose the pendulum at any angle, then release to let it swing. Sliders change the arm lengths, mass ratio, gravity, and trail persistence in real time. PAUSE and RUN hold or release the simulation (the space bar also toggles it), RESET returns to the default start, and TWIN adds a second pendulum offset by a thousandth of a radian so the visitor can watch the two diverge, both on the stage and in the phase portrait.
