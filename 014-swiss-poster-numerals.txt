Title: Raster 27 Poster

Description: A disciplined, poster-like single page for a fictional symposium about grids, built entirely from one sans-serif family, black, off-white and one red. A colossal "27" overprints a red square in the hero, oversized tabular numerals count up as each section enters view, and a live countdown runs in a black band. Everything sits on a visible-on-demand 12-column grid, so the page doubles as a lesson in the Swiss style it celebrates.

Visual techniques:
- A shared 12-column CSS grid with an asymmetric placement of every element
- Giant tight-tracked numerals with tabular figures and a multiply blend over a red block
- easeOutExpo requestAnimationFrame counters with zero padding and thousands separators
- IntersectionObserver-triggered staggered reveals and counter starts
- A fixed translucent grid overlay aligned with the layout and toggled by a button or the G key
- Hover fills rising from below and red rules drawn with scaleX
- Inverse pointer parallax on the hero block
- A live countdown computed from a fixed opening date

Interaction model: The reader scrolls through the poster as it assembles. Numbers count up as they appear, and hovering a statistic or the hero numeral replays its count. Programme rows invert to black when hovered or focused. The Grid button (or G) shows the twelve-column structure over the whole page, the red block in the hero drifts gently against the pointer, and the countdown keeps ticking live in the black band.
