Title: Bloom Mesh Gradient

Description: A vivid, poster-like colour study where a soft mesh gradient made from four blurred pools of pigment continuously drifts and recombines behind one enormous word. The type is set in overlay blend so the gradient blooms through the letters, edged by a fine outline ghost. Moving the pointer trails petals of luminous colour that swell and dissolve, a click releases a bigger blossom, and four curated palettes cross-fade the whole composition from pink peony to lagoon, ember and dusk.

Visual techniques:
- Pseudo mesh gradient from four large radial-gradient blobs under a heavy blur and saturation filter
- Independent keyframe drift loops with symmetric easing for continuous morphing
- @property-registered colour custom properties for smooth palette cross-fades
- Giant overlay-blended display type with a stroked ghost duplicate
- Pointer-spawned screen-blended ripple elements that self-remove on animationend
- Repeating radial-gradient grain overlay
- Frosted glass pill buttons with spring easing and pressed state; prefers-reduced-motion handling

Interaction model: Move the pointer to leave blooming ripples of the current palette's colours. Click or tap anywhere for a larger, staggered three-ring blossom. Choose Peony, Lagoon, Ember or Dusk from the palette pills, or use the left and right arrow keys, to cross-fade the whole mesh and text colour. Hovering the headline gently loosens its letter-spacing.
