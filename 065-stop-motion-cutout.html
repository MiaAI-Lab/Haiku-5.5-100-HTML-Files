The Little Tin Harbour

A cutout film shot one frame at a time on a kitchen table, with scissors, glue, and a borrowed lamp.

PROD.TIN HARBOUR
SCENE04 DAY
FRAME0000
RATE8 FPS

Tap the boat for a toot of paper steam. Every movement here happens in small jumps, the way it would under a camera, and every piece trembles slightly between frames, because hands are never perfectly still.
