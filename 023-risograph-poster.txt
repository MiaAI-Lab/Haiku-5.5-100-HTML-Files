Title: Riso Night Market Poster

Description: A tactile risograph print simulation. A bold three-ink poster for a fictional small-press night market is built entirely from CSS halftone screens, solid shapes and type, overprinted with multiply blending so pink over yellow makes coral and blue over pink makes violet. A print-shop job ticket lets you pull individual inks, loosen the registration and rerun the job pass by pass, so you can see how the image comes apart into its separations.

Visual techniques:
- Halftone dot screens from tiled radial-gradient backgrounds, rotated per ink to real screen angles
- mix-blend-mode: multiply overprint of three stacked ink layers
- Per-layer CSS custom-property transforms for randomized misregistration
- Pointer parallax that pulls the separations apart in different directions
- clip-path inset roller-wipe keyframes to simulate each print pass
- Mask-image gradient to fade the halftone sun
- Inline SVG feTurbulence paper grain multiplied over the sheet
- Em-based poster sizing tied to the viewport for exact responsive scaling
- Neo-brutalist job ticket with hard offset shadows and press micro-interactions

Interaction model: Hovering the poster shifts the three ink layers slightly against one another. Ink-drum buttons switch each colour on and off to show its separation. The Misregistration slider sets how far the layers drift, and "Jog the paper" re-randomizes their offsets. "Run the job" reprints the poster from blank, one ink pass at a time, while a status line reports progress.
