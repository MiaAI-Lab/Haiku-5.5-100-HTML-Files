Title: Squiggle Salon

Description: A loud, joyful Memphis-design poster page where the decoration is alive. Chunky outlined shapes in candy colours tumble in, squash against the floor, spin off walls and hop about behind a tilted yellow exhibition card. Clicking anywhere throws a burst of mini confetti shapes, any shape can be grabbed and flung, and a Shake button sends the whole room airborne. Polka dots, stripes, zigzags and hard offset shadows keep every element faithful to the 1980s Milan design vocabulary.

Visual techniques:
- Inline SVG shape library with shared pattern fills for dots and stripes
- Lightweight custom physics: gravity, restitution, friction, wall bounces and spin coupling
- Squash-and-stretch spring scaling anchored at each shape's base
- Hard offset shadows via CSS drop-shadow filters and box-shadows
- Radial-gradient polka-dot page background
- Pointer capture-style drag with velocity-based flinging
- Overshooting entrance animation and tactile button press states
- Body cap with oldest-confetti recycling to keep the DOM light

Interaction model: Click or tap anywhere on the background to burst a spray of small confetti shapes from that point. Press and drag any shape to pick it up and release to fling it with your pointer's velocity. "Throw confetti" fires a large burst from the top of the card and "Shake the room" kicks every shape into the air; the counter shows how many shapes are in play.
