Title: The Book of Lamplight

Description:
A warm, reverent single-page folio that feels like a page lifted from a medieval scriptorium. Cream vellum with fibrous texture sits under a moving pool of candlelight, wrapped in a glinting gilt frame. A lapis-and-gold illuminated initial grows its vines on load, the body reads in two justified columns of Palatino, and vermilion glosses and an ink-shy hidden verse reward slow, attentive reading.

Visual techniques:
- Layered radial and repeating-linear gradients for vellum, fibres, and vignette
- Inline SVG gilt border with an animated gradient stop for a glint
- SVG illuminated initial with stroke-dashoffset vine drawing and spring-scaled leaves
- CSS columns with a gilt column rule and shape-outside float for the initial
- Gradient mask transitions to make gloss notes appear as wiped ink
- Radial-gradient background-clip:text driven by a CSS custom property for the spreading ink reveal
- Pointer-eased radial lamplight via custom properties and requestAnimationFrame
- prefers-reduced-motion fallback that disables animation

Interaction model:
Moving the pointer drags a soft lamplight glow across the backdrop. Hovering, focusing, or tapping a glossed word underlines it in vermilion and reveals a marginal note; tapping elsewhere closes it. Resting the pointer or a finger on the hidden verse makes ink spread outward from that point until the whole verse is written; leaving pauses the spread.
